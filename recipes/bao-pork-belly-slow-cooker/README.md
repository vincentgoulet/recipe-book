# Slow cooker braised pork belly for bao

## Summary

Pork belly and flank braised in soy, Shaoxing wine and spices, finished in its own reduced glaze.

## Equipment

- Large pot
- Pan
- Slow cooker
- Strainer
- Wide saucepan

## Ingredients

### Braise

- 2.5 kg pork belly + flank, belly in 4–5 cm cubes, flank in 5–6 cm pieces
- 7 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 10–11 tbsp light soy sauce
- 500 ml Shaoxing wine
- 180–200 g granulated sugar
- 100 g fresh ginger, sliced
- 12 cloves garlic, crushed
- 5 star anise
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 1.2 L salted chicken broth
- 3 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil

### To serve

- bao buns
- quick pickled cucumber
- coriander
- spring onion
- crushed roasted peanuts *(optional)*

## Steps

### Braise

1. Boil the pork for 5 minutes. Drain and rinse.
2. In a pan over medium to medium-low heat, melt the sugar without stirring (swirl the pan) until deep amber.
3. Add the pork carefully (it splatters) and brown well on several sides, in batches.
4. Add the ginger and garlic; cook 1 minute.
5. Deglaze with the Shaoxing wine.
6. Add the soy sauces, oyster sauce, broth, star anise and cinnamon. Bring just to a simmer.
7. Transfer to the slow cooker. Cook on High for up to 4 h, until a chopstick slides in with almost no resistance.

### Glaze

8. Remove the pork. Strain the liquid into a wide saucepan.
9. Boil hard (8–9/10), stirring every minute or two, about 20–40 minutes, until reduced by 60–70% and it coats a spoon like maple syrup. Don't let it go candy-thick; loosen with a splash of hot water if it does.
10. Stir in the sesame oil. Taste; add light soy 1 tbsp at a time if needed.
11. Return the pork to the glaze for 5–10 minutes.

### Serve

12. Warm the bao (TBD).
13. Fill each: pork, a spoonful of glaze, pickled cucumber, coriander, spring onion, peanuts.
